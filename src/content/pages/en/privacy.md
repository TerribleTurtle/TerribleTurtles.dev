## What this site does

This site is a set of static pages. It does not set cookies, run analytics, track visitors, or have any forms or accounts. It does not ask for or store personal information.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes request data such as IP addresses, which it handles under its own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

If Cloudflare detects suspicious traffic, it may set strictly necessary security cookies to tell people apart from automated traffic. Those cookies come from Cloudflare's protection systems, not from this site.

## Links to other sites

Links to other sites, such as my projects or GitHub, lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

This site does not track you, so there is nothing to opt out of. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals need no special handling here.
